## THE FRAME: HOW THE BOOK CAME TO SOLOMON

In the name of Almighty God — living, true, everlasting, without end — I, Solomon, begin this book called Cephar Raziel, which contains seven complete treatises.

Glory and praise with great honor be to God, who made all things at one time. He is one God, mighty, alone, who was and is and shall always be. He has no equal and shall have none. He is singular without end, Lord alone without corruption, Holy, clean, humble, great, seeing all things, hearing all things, wise, and mighty in all things.

I begin this book so that whoever reads it may not blame it until they have read all — or at least part — of it. Then let them praise God, the maker of all things.

Solomon said: after I had ruled thirty years, on the fifth day of a Hebrew month when the sun was in Leo, there was sent to me from Babylon — from a Prince greater and more honored than any man of his time — a book called Cephar Raziel, which contains seven books and seven treatises.

The name of the Prince who sent it was Sameton. The two wise men who brought it were called Karmazail and Zazou. The name Raziel is interpreted as: *the great Angel of the secret of the Creator.*

In Hebrew, Cephar Raziel means the Book of Completion and Fulfillment. It was the first book written after Adam, in the language of Chaldaea, and afterward translated into Hebrew.

Know that in it is contained the Semiforas — the great name, complete, with all its component names and their virtues. I found in it seven books, seven treatises. The first and last were very obscure; the middle ones more clear. Although I found them dark, I expounded them as best I could.

Solomon said: who sees and does not understand is like one born blind who cannot distinguish colors. Who hears and does not comprehend is like a deaf man. Who speaks without being able to explain the reason is like a mute. Who considers without knowing what he is considering is like a drunk man. Who reads plain books without understanding is like one who dreams.

These proverbs Solomon offered so that a person would be careful with this book.

**Solomon said: Raziel the Angel said all of this to Adam, who was the first man in this world.**

## THE NINE PRECEPTS

These are the nine rules that govern the use of this book:

1. Do not suppose there is more than one — one singular Lord, alone above all things, who has none like him. Love him and honor him with all your heart, with good will, with steadiness and strength.

2. Do not live without law and without reason. If you do this, you will be loved by God your creator and by people.

3. Do not do to another what you would not want done to you. And treat strangers the same.

4. Do not lie to the Lord, nor to your friend. Say such truths as are to your benefit and not your harm.

5. Do not prefer or associate more with unwise people than with wise ones. Always love wisdom and good knowledge, and let your will and your life be in them.

6. Do not speak before you have thought. Consider in your heart what you will do before you do it.

7. Do not reveal your secrets to a woman, or to a child, or to a fool, or to a drunk man.

8. Do not test a medicine or a poison on yourself or another without cause.

9. Do not condemn a book, a prophecy, or a wise person until you have tested them.

If you hold these nine precepts, you will profit more and more. For after understanding and knowledge, after capacity and true will, all things yield to good wit and good discretion.

## BOOK I: CLAVIS — THE KEY
### On the Seven Planets and the Structure of the Cosmos

**The key of this book is to know the places of the seven bodies above — their natures, their sciences, their properties, and all their virtues as they appear to us on earth.**

Solomon described this with a figure: a key whose shaft represents the one singular God; whose four-square cross-piece represents the four elements; whose triangle represents the human being in body, soul, and spirit. The seven wards of the key are the seven Angels who have power over the seven heavens and the seven days of the week. These, said Solomon, together govern all the world, in heights and in depths.

### The Seven Brothers

There is one father who has seven sons. These seven are siblings born of the same father. The eldest is heaviest of all. The one in the middle is most orderly among them. The others share fellowship continuously. These seven brothers are the seven planets. Solomon named them:

| Hebrew Name | Planet | Nature |
|-------------|--------|--------|
| Sabaday | Saturn | Cold and dry; melancholic; signifies fathers, discord, and wrath in lands |
| Zedek | Jupiter | Hot and moist; sanguine; sweet in favor; signifies good, honor, and virtue |
| Madin | Mars | Hot and dry; evil; a fighter, ravisher, deceiver |
| Hamina | Sol (Sun) | Middle among all; healthy and mighty; all others defer to and fear him; truthful and strong |
| Noga | Venus | Cold and moist; pleasant; phlegmatic; well-formed; glad among women; signifies good things |
| Cocab | Mercury | Serves all and holds himself with all; signifies writers, tidings, and voices |
| Labana | Luna (Moon) | Cold and moist; signifies kinship; carries the commands of the planets each month and delivers them |

These seven planets govern the seven climates of the world. They have power over all beasts formed here, over the four elements, over the four times of the year, and over the four natures with their complexions. The prophets called them seven living and holy spirits. The wise called them seven burning lamps, seven candlesticks of light and life.

### The Seven as Foundation

If any one of the seven were absent, the earth would return to its first state when all the elements were confusion. If any of them were to receive corruption, everything that receives a soul or life would be destroyed.

Saturn holds the earth in balance so it is not moved. Jupiter holds the air. Mars the fire. The Sun holds the day and humanity. Venus holds the fairer parts of the world. Mercury holds Reason. The Moon holds the earth, sea, and waters and their powers.

### The Twelve Signs and Their Natures

The seven brothers divide the twelve realms of the zodiac among them. Within each realm there are thirty cities, within each city sixty castles, within each castle sixty territories. The structure is fractal, from cosmos to region to district to place.

The four temperaments of the signs:
- **Aries, Leo, Sagittarius**: Hot and dry, choleric — power in the East
- **Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn**: Earthy, cold, dry, melancholic — power in the South
- **Gemini, Libra, Aquarius**: Hot and moist, sanguine — power in the West
- **Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces**: Watery, feminine, moist, phlegmatic — power in the North

### The Key of Astronomical Navigation

The key of this book is the knowledge of the seven planetary bodies — their places, natures, sciences, and judgments. Without this knowledge, nothing else in the book can be understood or used.

Solomon warned: do not work by this book in vain, without reason. If you work without reason, if you are unclean or evil in yourself, you may harm yourself rather than accomplish what you seek. The book has power; that power requires preparation.

## BOOK II: ALAE — THE WINGS
### On Stones, Herbs, Beasts, and the Twenty-Two Letters

Solomon said: as wings carry a bird to where it desires to go, so by the virtues of stones, herbs, and beasts you may attain what you seek — if you understand their natures, properties, and virtues. This book is called Wings because without wings no bird or fish can move.

The four wings are the four key virtues of this book: stones, herbs, beasts, and letters.

### Wing One: The Twenty-Four Stones

The book was given to Solomon crowned with seven stones of great power, set in it by seven Angels who have power over the seven heavens and the seven days of the week. From these seven, the system of twenty-four extends — one stone for each hour of the day and night.

The principle: every stone's virtue is determined by its nature and its celestial correspondence. Any person who bears a stone must be pure and clean. The stones do not act by themselves — they amplify what is brought to them.

Selected correspondences:
- **Ruby**: Clarity, brightness; makes good color in those who bear it reverently; increases goods
- **Sapphire**: Pure blue as clean heaven; heals inflammatory conditions; brings honor of great lords
- **Emerald**: Green, fair; increases riches; enables prophecy of things to come when worn in gold
- **Beryl**: Color of sea water; warms the closed hand; creates friendship between two people when touched
- **Amethyst**: Color of wine or violet; chases away spirits; defends against excess

### Wing Two: The Twenty-Four Herbs

Selected correspondences:
- **Rosemary** (*Corona regia*): Good odor; warms and sharpens the brain; suffumed in a house, it drives away evil spirits
- **Artemesia** (mugwort): Mother and first of herbs; used in all workings; conjures winds and spirits
- **Fennel**: Holy herb; medicine for the eyes; good light; drives away evil spirits and evil eyes
- **Sage**: Disperses evil shades and spirits from any place it is; beneficial to bear with you

### Wing Three: The Beasts — Of Earth, Air, Water, and Fire

The six categories of non-physical entities:

1. **Pure fire-beings**: Clear, bright as the beams of the sun; like flame or a spark or a star; natural to them in the way that lightning is to the sky
2. **Air-beings**: Darker than the first; taking form through water, cloud, moisture, or darkness; speaking through sound or the shape of an animal
3. **Threshold-beings**: They accompany the spirit, especially the corpulent or thick of spirit; found by night in places of fear; sometimes seen as the souls of the recently dead in churchyards
4. **The Wind**: Heard daily but unseen; hot when from east or south, cold when from west or north; bears clouds and waters and tempests; stirs and breaks; called "living air" as fire above is called "living fire"
5. **Fantasies**: Shades of various colors and forms, compounded together; appearing in desert places or corrupt air; sometimes descending from hills as knights, sometimes upon waters as fair women
6. **Demons**: Descended from heights to depths; formed of pure matter without corruption; permanent; knowing all things; dwelling in darkness; able to take any form by the will of the Creator

Raziel said upon these six: invocation, appearance, constraining, binding, loosing, and doing good or evil by them — all are accomplished with cleanliness, prayer, fasting, suffumigation, and praise of God.

Know that if you can transpose these twenty-two letters or figures as it is fitting, you will attain the great name of the Creator. With it you may do what you will — with cleanliness and with the help of the Creator.

These twenty-two letters are the key that unlocks everything in this book. With them all names are formed; with them all things are bound and loosed.

## BOOK III: THYMIAMATA — THE SUFFUMIGATIONS
### On the Seven Incenses, One for Each Planet

Solomon said: as there are seven heavens, seven stars, and seven days in the week — each distinct from the others and not alike — so there are seven suffumigations, which hold in themselves the virtue of the seven stars and make glad the spirits of the air, the Angels of the heavens, and the spirits of the earth.

When a person offers what belongs to each spirit, the spirits are appeased. They become present and willing when called cleanly by name. This is why suffumigation is paired with prayer: the prayer names them, the incense calls them.

| Planet | Day | Primary Incense |
|--------|-----|----------------|
| Saturn | Saturday | All good-smelling roots; herb of frankincense |
| Sun | Sunday | Frankincense, mastic, musk, and all good gums and resins |
| Moon | Monday | Leaves of myrtle, bay, and all fragrant leaves |
| Mars | Tuesday | Red, black, and white sandalwood; aloes-wood; cypress |
| Mercury | Wednesday | All barks and rinds: cinnamon, cassia, citrus rind, mace |
| Jupiter | Thursday | Nutmeg, cloves, citron; dried and powdered orange rind; all fragrant fruits |
| Venus | Friday | Flowers: rose, violet, saffron, and all sweet-smelling flowers |

Each fragrant incense gathers its spirits according to its nature and its strength. Good draws good; better draws better. Evil incense drives away good spirits and makes even evil spirits fearful — because every thing loves health more than sickness.

The complete incense is made from all seven: root (Saturn), gum (Sun), leaf (Moon), wood (Mars), rind (Mercury), fruit (Jupiter), flower (Venus). This is the incense of Luna, the gathering incense, for it contains a portion of every planet. It is called *Thymiama completum*.

Hermes said: there is no such suffumigation for calling spirits as amber, aloes-wood, costus, musk, saffron, and blood of a lapwing with frankincense. These are the food and drink and gladness of the spirits of the air. They gather them together strongly and quickly.

## BOOK IV: ON TIMES
### When Each Work Should Be Done

The fourth treatise establishes when each working should be performed: in which hour of the day, which day of the week, which month, which season.

Solomon's warning: just as a son cannot ask something of his father in every hour and be given it, so you cannot work at every moment. There are hours of each planet, and the planet's nature determines what is favorable in that hour.

The hours cycle through the planets continuously. In the day of Saturn, the first hour belongs to Saturn, the second to Jupiter, the third to Mars, the fourth to the Sun, the fifth to Venus, the sixth to Mercury, the seventh to the Moon — then the cycle repeats.

All secret matters should be asked in Saturn's hour, with all depth. All honor and substance in Jupiter's. All strife and battle in Mars's. All clarity and lordship in the Sun's. All beauty, love, and richness in Venus's. All reason, wit, and subtlety in Mercury's. All change and fortune in the Moon's.

Know that from these seven you must always take counsel where you find them in their houses and signs.

## BOOK V: ON CLEANLINESS
### The Discipline of Preparation

Solomon said to the Angel Nathanael: which are those seven bright bodies that never stop in their going? Their ways are never void and they never fail — only enduring, going their ways.

Nathanael said to Solomon: The seven bright bodies you see above are placed beneath as governors, and in their movement they hold the four elements beneath them in balance. Their matter is simple and pure, without corruption, always durable. Their condition is likened to the Lord who formed them, though they obey him in all things.

In the heavens of these seven bright bodies there are Angels without number, mighty in all things. Each has their proper power, strength, and virtue. Each serves their office to the Creator who formed them.

Solomon asked: what is the life, power, and service of these Angels?

Nathanael said: their life is of cleanliness, prayer, and faithfulness. Their power comes from suffumigation, offering, and sacrifice. Their service: when the Creator wills it, they go from place to place when any clean person has prayed to the Creator as it should be done. Then they do good or evil as the Creator wills, for in them is power, knowledge, and complete will.

What is necessary for working with the Semiforas:
- Humility
- Fasting
- Prayer
- Trust
- Charity
- Cleanliness
- Patience
- Steadiness

These seven above all: in humility, in trust, patience, abstinence, faithfulness, charity, mercy — these must be present in the one who works.

Know the seven planets and their natures. Know when to ask what of each one. Know the waxing and decreasing of the moon, by which all creatures in the sea, rivers, and wells, and all bodies beneath, and blood in the veins, wax and decrease. Trust the virtue of the Moon to work.

## BOOK VII: THE VIRTUES — THE GREAT NAMES
### The Semiforas of Adam and Moses

The Semiforas is the central name, the root, the beginning, and foundation of all prayer. It is the exemplar of good life, the trust of the human body, the prayer of a just person.

Solomon said above Semiforas: these seven qualities are necessary in the one who works — humility, fasting, prayer, trust, charity, cleanliness, patience. A person must not work with the Semiforas except with great right and law and much reason.

### The Semiforas of Adam: Seven Chapters

Adam was given seven modes of speech — seven ways of knowing — by the Creator:

1. **Speech with the Creator**: Adam spoke with the Creator in paradise
2. **Speech with Angels**: Adam spoke with Angels and received from them written letters
3. **Speech with Devils and the Dead**: Adam spoke with devils and with dead men, taking counsel from them
4. **Binding and Loosing of Beasts and Spirits**: Adam bound and loosed living creatures
5. **Speech with Seeds, Herbs, and Trees**: Adam spoke with growing things
6. **Speech with Winds and the Four Elements**: Adam spoke with the elemental forces
7. **Speech with the Sun, Moon, and Stars**: Adam spoke with the celestial bodies

By these seven virtues of Semiforas, whatever Adam would do, he did. Whatever he would destroy, he destroyed. This Semiforas Adam had when the Creator breathed grace into him.

### The Semiforas of Moses: Seven Chapters

Moses received a corresponding seven-chapter structure:

1. When Moses ascended the mountain and spoke with the flame surrounding the bush that seemed to burn but did not burn
2. When Moses spoke with the Creator on the mountain
3. When Moses divided the Red Sea and passed through it
4. When the staff was changed into a serpent, and the serpent devoured the others
5. The names written in the forehead of Aaron
6. When Moses made the brazen serpent and overcame the plagues of Egypt
7. When Moses caused manna to rain in the desert, drew water from the rock, and led the children of Israel out of captivity

### The Names

Solomon found in the Ark of the Covenant:

- The book called Semiphoras which the Creator gave to Adam in paradise
- A second book given to Adam in paradise in the hour of necessity
- The book the Creator gave to Moses on the mount of Sinai after Moses had fulfilled his fasting

He also found: a pot full of manna; the staff of Moses which had been changed into a serpent and back; the Tablets of the Law; golden tablets with fifteen precious stones bearing the High Holy Names; a box of green marble containing seven figures, each figure bearing seven great and virtuous names — some given to Moses, some taught to Adam in paradise.

Around the extremities of the Ark: twenty-four rings with the names and figures of the Creator written in diverse colors.

Solomon said: I found in one of those rings such virtue that when I said "make it rain," it rained. When I said "make the rain cease," it ceased. And throughout the region of Jerusalem, both tempests and rains obeyed.

## STRUCTURAL SUMMARY: THE ARCHITECTURE OF SEVENS

The Sefer Raziel is organized around the number seven at every level:

| Scale | Seven |
|-------|-------|
| Cosmic | 7 Heavens |
| Celestial | 7 Planets |
| Temporal | 7 Days of the week |
| Elemental | 7 Suffumigations |
| Material | 7 Stones of Raziel's crown |
| Linguistic | 22 letters (root of all names) |
| Human | Body, soul, and spirit × the 4 elements |
| Divine transmission | 7 chapters of Adam's Semiforas; 7 chapters of Moses's Semiforas |
| Angelic hierarchy | 7 ruling angels, each over one heaven and one planet |

The book makes explicit that these sevens are not separate systems but one system viewed from different angles. The seven planets are the seven days are the seven heavens are the seven modes of speech are the seven incenses. One father, seven sons, one inheritance distributed seven ways.

The key image Solomon uses: a castle with many gates, each requiring its own key. The seven planets are the seven keys. Without knowing their places, natures, sciences, and properties, the gates of the cosmos remain locked.
